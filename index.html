Smart Spending: Payment Trade-Offs & Cash Liquidity

Most college money mistakes do not happen from reckless splurges—they happen when an everyday payment tool collides with an unverified seller, a group bill split, a pre-authorization hold, or an overnight auto-debit. Practice pairing the right payment method with its underlying trade-off, protect checking liquidity across multi-step scenarios, and verify independent transfer when structural scaffolds fade.

Carried-Over Learning Objectives (Replicated & Extended from S26 Baseline)
  • LO1 Compare debit cards, credit cards, and mobile payment apps in real-life college scenarios.
  • LO2 Evaluate payment methods across four trade-off dimensions: cost/fees, convenience, security/fraud protection, and budgeting/liquidity control.
  • LO3 Recognize how psychological factors (pain of paying, deferred payment illusion, reward-point chasing) influence spending choices.
  • LO4 Apply multi-step payment decision strategies to protect cash liquidity and avoid overdraft fees.

Payment Method Cost & Fees Convenience Security & Fraud Protection Budgeting, Liquidity & Psychology
Debit Card
Checking-Linked		 $0 transaction fee, but $35 overdraft risk if balance drops below $0. Universal POS & ATM access; poor for multi-person bill splits. Reg E limits liability if reported quickly, but disputed bank cash stays frozen up to 10 business days. High pain of paying curbs impulse drift, yet consumes liquid checking cash needed for upcoming auto-debits.
Credit Card
Paid in Full		 $0 interest when statement is paid in full; 22%–29% APR if carried; cash advances incur upfront fees. Accepted online & in-store; absorbs hotel/fuel pre-auth holds on credit line. Strongest shield (FCBA): issuer's funds—not your checking cash—are held during merchant disputes. Short-term grace-period float buffers checking liquidity, but deferred payment illusion & point-chasing inflate spending.
Mobile P2P App
Venmo / Cash App / Zelle		 $0 bank/balance transfers; ~3% fee if funded by credit card; ~1.75% instant cash-out fee. Fastest exact peer-to-peer bill splitting among classmates you know and trust. Authorized push transfers act like handing over cash—virtually zero chargeback recourse with strangers. Bank-linked sends still drain checking cash immediately; money left sitting in app balances does not cover bank auto-pays.
